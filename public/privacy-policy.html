Privacy Policy — Sixer Pro Cricket 3D

Effective Date: 2026

Sixer Pro Cricket 3D respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles information when you play on mobile or web.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All gameplay progress — including coins, career stats, high-score leaderboard entries, tournament progress, settings, and any custom 3D (.GLB) models you choose to import — is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). We do not upload or sell your personal data.

2. Advertisements & Third-Party Links

The game may display sponsor banners or links configured by the game administrator. Clicking an external sponsor or live-score link opens a third-party website governed by that site's own privacy policy.

3. Children's Privacy

Sixer Pro Cricket 3D does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

4. Permissions

The app does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or precise GPS location to play.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing.
